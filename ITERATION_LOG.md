# Iteration log

1. **Architecture preservation:** extracted business invariants from the prior implementation; retained server pricing, payment verification, RLS, guest hashing and adapter-based fulfillment.
2. **Framework migration:** replaced Astro pages with Next.js App Router server/client boundaries and OpenNext deployment.
3. **Design reset:** replaced the restrained template-like composition with night/paper worlds, cinematic hero, asymmetric edit, scent-family navigation, sample-first PDP and editorial typography.
4. **Commerce depth:** added discovery credit reservation/consumption, gift mode, note vocabulary, quiz, account and admin views.
5. **Truthfulness pass:** labelled placeholder product art, made legal/merchant approvals explicit, left all live fulfillment disabled by default.
6. **QA harness:** added source invariants, unit/E2E tests, CI and performance-budget configuration. CI remains the authority for install/build because the local workspace cannot reach npm.
